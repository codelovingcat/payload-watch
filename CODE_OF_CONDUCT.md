# Code of Conduct

PayloadWatch aims to maintain a small, welcoming, and technically focused developer community. This is a lightweight project conduct policy; it does not change software licensing or grant reuse rights.

## Expected behavior

- Treat everyone respectfully regardless of background, experience level, identity, or viewpoint.
- Keep code reviews specific, constructive, and evidence-based. Assume good faith, but be willing to revise your position.
- Welcome questions and provide actionable feedback rather than personal criticism.
- Respect the privacy of contributors and users. Do not disclose personal details, credentials, screenshots of private conversations, or real API payloads without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, targeted insults, intimidation, stalking, or publication of private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruption or bad-faith engagement is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For ordinary, non-sensitive project discussions, open a GitHub issue or comment with the relevant context. Do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue. For harassment, threats, or abuse, use GitHub's built-in content or user reporting tools to report privately to GitHub. A dedicated private project reporting contact has not been established; we should add one before adopting a more formal community enforcement process.

The repository maintainer may ask participants to change behavior, remove inappropriate comments where permissions allow, lock threads, decline contributions, or restrict abusive participation, consistent with GitHub's platform rules. Reports should be handled respectfully and without retaliation. This policy may be refined as the contributor community grows.
